The county media and communications team look after the county's social media pages and share stories about the adventures and opportunities our members and volunteers have experienced to inspire others and attract new members.

Members of the team often attend guiding events to take photos and videos to share but would also love to hear from you directly about what you, your unit, district, or division have been up to.

We want to celebrate your successes, fun times and inspire others to try new challenges and ideas.

Tell us in your own words about your exciting experiences, along with photograph(s) and relevant **group photo consent** (covers girls featured where the person completing the form can check consents on GO) using the 'Submit a story' form below:

[Submit a story](https://forms.office.com/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=3yob_CzTykeMNWNnWM6OwWD1qHUtWO5Aip3HqVte2p9URUlTVkozSFZGUEM3MDlGWVJCS1EyNDMwTCQlQCN0PWcu&route=shorturl){:target="_blank"}{:rel="noopener"}{:.btn.btn-primary.external-link}

Adults featured in a photograph, will need to submit their consent separately via the online **individual photo consent** form on the [Forms webpage](/about-us/resources/#individual-consent).

The individual consent form can also be used by a parent/guardian/carer to provide consent for their child, aged under 14, if attending an event without their leader.
