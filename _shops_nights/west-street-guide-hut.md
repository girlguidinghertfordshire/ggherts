### Indoor accommodation

Main hall sleeps up to 24 girls, air beds can be provided, as well as lightweight indoor tents which fit 2 airbeds in each tent.

Pauline Geering Room provides leader accommodation for 4 on two bunk beds.

Ranger Room also has a double sofa bed for extra volunteer accommodation.

### Facilities

- Main hall has chairs, tables and projection screen.
- Well equipped kitchen with serving hatch into hall.
- Accessible front entrance, hall and toilet.
- Girlguiding users have access to a well equipped games store.
- Outside, located in our secure grassed area, there is a large campfire circle. Altar fires are also available for patrol cooking.
- Large public access fields next to the hut for outdoor games.

Please see the website for photos, availability, hire rates and booking enquiry.
