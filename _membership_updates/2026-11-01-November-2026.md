### Safer guiding and disclosure checks

Please encourage volunteers to complete their safer guiding training, and disclosure checks as early as possible. Disclosures, in particular, are currently experiencing longer processing times. Please encourage volunteers due to renew in November to start the process as soon as possible.

### Going away with and catering workbook scheme retirement

The old going away with and catering workbook schemes will retire on **30 November**. If you're working through one of the **retiring schemes**, please make sure it's **signed off** and **marked as completed** on GO by **30 November**. After this date, the workbooks and accompanying resources will be retired from the Girlguiding online shop and **any modules still in progress will automatically be marked as abandoned on GO.** The scheme will then be fully digital, apart from the travel abroad module. Travel abroad will be made available as its own workbook and will continue to be a physical resource.

### Improvements to the leader development programme (LDP)

- A new [leader development programme page](https://www.girlguiding.org.uk/information-for-volunteers/learning-and-development/leader-development-programme/){:.external-link}{:rel="noopener"}{:target="_blank"} is now on the Girlguiding website.
- A new welcome page, simpler registration process, a single webinars page, and a refreshed eLearning for managing unit accounts on the [learning platform](https://learning.girlguiding.org.uk/login/index.php){:.external-link}{:rel="noopener"}{:target="_blank"}.
- A new resource bank with everything needed in one place, including a new [leader development programme guide](https://www.girlguiding.org.uk/globalassets/docs-and-resources/learning-and-development/ldp-new-resources/ldp-guide.pdf){:.download-link}{:rel="noopener"}{:target="_blank"} which covers everything volunteers need to get started.
- More support for volunteers who started their LDP on or before 1 September 2024 and are still in progress.
