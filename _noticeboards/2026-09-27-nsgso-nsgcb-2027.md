Applications are now open for the 2027 NSGSO and NSGCB course taking place from Sunday 1 - Sunday 8 August.

Do you play an orchestral or concert band instrument to grade 4 or above and will be aged between 13 and 25 by 31 August 2027?

Applicants between the ages of 26 - 30 may be considered in the event that a section is undersubscribed.

Why not spend a week during the summer in Stourbridge, West Midlands, working alongside other talented musicians rehearsing, socialising and attending workshops. All leading up to taking part in concerts in the Birmingham area at the end of the week.

Cost: £650, inclusive of all food, accommodation, tuition, music, social activities and transport during the course.

Closing date for applications is 9am on Sunday 29 November.

Applicants will find out mid December whether they have been successful or not. A deposit of £95 is required to secure your place if you are successful.

Find out more and apply via the [NSGSO-NSGCB website](https://nsgso-nsgcb.com/the-cource/){:.external-link}{:rel="noopener"}{:target="_blank"}
