Our 'festival fever' county camp 2026 has now taken place.

We hope all had an amazing time and will cherish the memories forever.

Over 700 members attended throughout the week, across all sections and age groups.

A huge thank you to all those who came along and the helpers who made it possible (before, during and after - behind the scenes and onsite throughout).

Keep an eye out for photos and articles on our social media pages as to what we got up to.
