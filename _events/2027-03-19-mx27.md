Bookings are now open for MX27!

Taking place from Friday 19 - Sunday 21 March 2027 this annual mountain experience weekend always proves very popular.

A fun, but challenging, residential weekend, aimed at Rangers, young leaders and adults (leaders do not need to attend with girls) is for anyone wanting to learn higher level map and compass skills whilst staying cosily indoors overnight.

Fully catered, transport included, £95 per person.

Good for personal skills, DofE training, Peak Assault training, Lowland leader or Girlguiding Walking Scheme training.

Places are very limited.

Book via email: <walking@girlguidinghertfordshire.org.uk>
