This ever popular weekend is coming back in 2027!

Taking place in the Peak District from Friday 30 April until Monday 3 May, this jam packed camping weekend, full of fun and adventure, includes caving, outdoor climbing, cycling and hill walking for Guides and Scouts aged 11-13 years.

[Event flyer](/assets/docs/2026/itth-event-flyer-2027.pdf){:.btn.btn-primary}{:target="_blank"}{:rel="noopener"}

Bookings open **Thursday 1 October** and places always go quickly, so please let your Guides know about this now so they can act fast in the autumn to secure a spot and have a real adventure!
