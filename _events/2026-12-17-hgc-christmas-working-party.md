Why not put on your Christmas jumper and Santa hat and come and spread some festive cheer at the county centre in Cottered on Thursday 17 December for a festive working party.

Take a break from shopping and wrapping and help pack up the HGC kitchen and management cupboard (no bows or ribbons needed) ready for building works to commence in early 2027.

Mince pies, tea and coffee will of course be provided.

Bring your own lunch if you are staying for the day.

Even Santa needs helpers, so please do come along and lend any time you can spare between 10am and 4pm.

Email <janetacc@girlguidinghertfordshire.org.uk> or <hgcchair@girlguidinghertfordshire.org.uk> to let us know to expect you.

(WhatsApp also agreeable if you have their numbers)
