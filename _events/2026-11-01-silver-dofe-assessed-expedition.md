Having completed all of your training earlier in the year, you are now ready to head off for your Silver assessed expedition.

This weekend is taking place in the beautiful Peak District.

(Dates to be confirmed once teams are formed)

Leaving early on Friday morning (a day off school will be required) you will start the weekend with the first of 3 days of navigating, exploring and walking before setting up camp each night and having well deserved hot dinners cooked by you!

After breakfast and packing up, the adventure continues onwards for another 2 full days of walking to complete your expedition and find your way to the finish.

Cost: £120 (includes transport, site fees, gas, and camping equipment).

For more information, or to book a place, please contact the county DofE Award lead volunteer on <dofe@girlguidinghertfordshire.org.uk>

More information about DofE within Girlguiding can also be found on our [DofE page](/youth-opportunities/dofe/).

If you are unable to make these dates, please advise the county DofE Award lead volunteer as soon as possible so that other counties can be contacted to see what opportunities they have available.

Gold Expeditions are arranged via Girlguiding Anglia – please contact <angliadofe@girlguiding-anglia.org.uk> for more information.
