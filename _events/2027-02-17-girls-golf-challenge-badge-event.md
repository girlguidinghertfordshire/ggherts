Looking for something to do during the February half term?

Our young members aged 7+ (Brownies, Guides, Rangers and young leaders) are invited to take part in the next Girls Golf challenge badge event, taking place on Wednesday 17 February from 1.30pm-4pm at Essendon Golf Club in Hatfield (AL9 6HN).

During the afternoon girls will complete activities relating to putting, chipping and driving, the key introductory elements of playing golf.

The session will be led by a Golf Professional Coach connected with Herts Golf/Essendon Golf Club.

Cost: £5 (information about payment is set out in the consent form).

All girls who complete the session will earn their [Girls Golf challenge badge](/unit/badges/#girls-golf-challenge-badge).

Leaders don't need to attend with their girls, but are welcome to join them if they like.

Please let your girls know about this fun opportunity.

Parents/carers of girls wishing to take part will need to download and complete the below consent form (which should then be returned to <nicola1.day@hotmail.co.uk> via their child's unit leader) before Friday 29 January.
