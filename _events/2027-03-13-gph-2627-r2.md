Save the date!

The annual county Great Patrol Hunt, for Guides aged 10 -15 years old, has commenced!

The competition spans a year and comprises of 3 rounds.

Each round has different and diverse challenges that encourage patrols to work together as a team. Marks are awarded for team work, presentation, effort, etiquette, good deeds and achievement throughout the challenges and the event.

Don't worry if you didn't participate in round 1 in October 2026 though, you can still join in the fun for round 2 and 3.

Booking information and venue will be announced in coming months.

For more information visit the [GPH's webpage.](/great-patrol-hunt/)
