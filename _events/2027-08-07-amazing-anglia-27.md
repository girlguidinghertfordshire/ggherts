There's still time for Guides, Rangers and young leaders to apply to attend Amazing Anglia 27 - Together in harmony, an exciting region camp at Hautbois activity centre in Norwich.

Young member applications have been extended until **30 October 2026**.

### What's included

- 6 nights camping
- Breakfast, lunch and dinner throughout the event
- Adventurous activities
- Craft and wellbeing workshops
- Evening entertainment
- Event badge and merchandise

Anglia 27 offers a fantastic mix of experiences throughout the week. Activities include adventurous challenges, traditional camp skills, wellness sessions, creative crafts, music and drama.

Girls will take part in exciting activity sessions each day in small groups creating a great opportunity to build teamwork, confidence, and lasting friendships throughout the week.

**Cost:** £400pp (including coach from/to locations in Hertfordshire - tbc)
