## Help make a difference!

Does your company allocate days for staff to volunteer to help charities, non-profits or community projects?

Why not come along and join us for a working party day at one of the guiding properties in Hertfordshire county.

Help from external organisations plays an important part in enabling us to keep guiding properties looking fresh and modern, and ensure they can continue to be used by our members into the future.

There are tasks to suit everyone, no matter your skill level; be it decorating, gardening, organising or general odd jobs.

We are grateful for all and any help. So if you can spare a whole day or even just a few hours, we'd love to hear from you.

Get in touch with Janet Eavis, assistant county commissioner - membership support, to discuss how your company can support Girlguiding Hertfordshire.
