Save the date - Saturday 11 September

Why not join the county walking team for more Hertfordshire Way social walks.

Officially opened in July 1998 and to celebrate its upcoming 30th anniversary the walking team are inviting all members to join them in walking a little bit (or several little bits) of the Hertfordshire Way before July 2028.

Explore the beauty of our county on foot whilst building your confidence outdoors and then take your unit or young members out and about on a Hertfordshire Way walking route near you?
