This weekend, taking place on Saturday 23 to Sunday 24 January, is for everyone intending to undertake practice, or assessed, Duke of Edinburgh (DofE) expeditions with Girlguiding in 2027.

All the main areas of Bronze expedition training will be covered, plus Silver DofE participants will learn to use eDofE, OS maps and plan their practice expedition. We will revise higher level navigation skills, first aid, food and expedition aims.

This weekend will also be a great opportunity for participants to meet the other members of their future expedition teams.

Cost: £50 (includes accommodation, food and resources).

For more information, or to book a place, please contact the county DofE Award lead volunteer on <dofe@girlguidinghertfordshire.org.uk>

More information about DofE within Girlguiding can also be found on our [DofE page](/youth-opportunities/dofe/).

## What next?

### Bronze DofE

- [Training expedition](/event/bronze-dofe-training-expedition/) - Saturday 6 - Sunday 7 March, Broxbourne, Herts
- [Assessed expedition](/event/bronze-dofe-assessed-expedition/) - Saturday 24 - Sunday 25 April, the Chilterns

### Silver DofE

- [Training expedition](/event/silver-dofe-practice-expedition/) - Friday 23 - Sunday 25 April, the Chilterns
- [Assessed expedition](/event/silver-dofe-assessed-expedition/) - Dates tbc, Peak District

If you are unable to make these dates, please advise the county DofE Award lead volunteer as soon as possible so that other counties can be contacted to see what opportunities they have available.

Gold Expeditions are arranged via Girlguiding Anglia – please contact <angliadofe@girlguiding-anglia.org.uk> for more information.
