Having completed your indoor training earlier in the year, head off for your Silver practice expedition on Friday 23 to Sunday 25 April in the Chilterns for a weekend adventure of navigating, exploring and walking from Friday evening until Sunday, with 2 night camps to pitch up your tents and enjoy warm meals prepared by yourself, before breakfast to set you off afresh for the day ahead.

Cost: £40 (includes site fees, gas, equipment and petrol for supervisors).

For more information, or to book a place, please contact the county DofE Award lead volunteer on <dofe@girlguidinghertfordshire.org.uk>

More information about DofE within Girlguiding can also be found on our [DofE page](/youth-opportunities/dofe/).

Once completed you are all set for your [Silver assessed expedition](/event/silver-dofe-assessed-expedition/) in the Peak District (dates to be confirmed once teams formed).

If you are unable to make these dates, please advise the county DofE Award lead volunteer by emailing <dofe@girlguidinghertfordshire.org.uk> as soon as possible so that other counties can be contacted to see what opportunities they have available.

Gold Expeditions are arranged via Girlguiding Anglia – please contact <angliadofe@girlguiding-anglia.org.uk> for more information.
