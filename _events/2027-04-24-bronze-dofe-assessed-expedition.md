Having completed all of your training earlier in the year, you are now ready to head off for your Bronze assessed expedition on Saturday 24 to Sunday 25 April.

This weekend is taking place in the beautiful Chilterns.

Saturday is a day of navigating, exploring and walking before setting up camp for the night and having a well deserved dinner cooked by you!

After breakfast and packing up, the adventure continues onwards for another full day of walking to complete your expedition and find your way to the finish.

Cost: £30 (includes site fees, gas, equipment and petrol for supervisors).

For more information, or to book a place, please contact the county DofE Award lead volunteer on <dofe@girlguidinghertfordshire.org.uk>

More information about DofE within Girlguiding can also be found on our [DofE page](/youth-opportunities/dofe/).

If you are unable to make these dates, please advise the county DofE Award lead volunteer as soon as possible so that other counties can be contacted to see what opportunities they have available.

Gold Expeditions are arranged via Girlguiding Anglia – please contact <angliadofe@girlguiding-anglia.org.uk> for more information.
