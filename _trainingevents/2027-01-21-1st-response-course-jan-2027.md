This 1st response course, for those requiring the qualification, is taking place over 2 evening sessions, **Thursday 21 January** and **Thursday 4 February** at The Guide Hut, 2A Townsend Lane, Harpenden, AL5 2QE. Both sessions will run from 7pm - 10pm (arrival from 6.30pm). **Both evenings must be attended to complete the course.**

**Cost:** £20 (non-refundable). The course organiser will contact you regarding payment.

Tea and coffee will be provided but please bring your own lunch

Uniform is optional.

Members from outside Hertfordshire county interested in attending this training may book on to it **only in the 2 weeks immediately prior to the course date,** if spaces are available.
