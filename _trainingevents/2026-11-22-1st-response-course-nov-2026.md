This 1st response course, taking place at the Guide HQ, Scrubbits Park Road, Radlett, WD7 8JN on **Sunday 22 November** (10am - 4pm), is for any Hertfordshire county members or volunteers requiring the qualification.

Tea and coffee will be provided but please do bring your own lunch and cold drinks. Uniform is optional.

**Cost:** £20 (non-refundable) payable in advance. Payments by bank transfer to the First Aid Fundraising account (account details set out in confirmation of booking email so please keep an eye on your inbox and check your junk/spam folders).

Bookings close **Monday 16 November.**

Guiding members from outside Hertfordshire county, interested in attending this training, are asked to book only in the 2 weeks immediately prior to the course date, if spaces are available.
